## Concepts Covered

- Retaining wall design parameters and constraints
- ML surrogate models for rapid design evaluation
- Multi-objective optimization: cost vs. safety factor
- Pareto front analysis for design tradeoffs
- Constraint handling for geotechnical design requirements

## Key Takeaways

1. ML surrogate models enable rapid evaluation of thousands of design alternatives that would be infeasible with detailed analysis.
2. Multi-objective optimization reveals the tradeoff between construction cost and safety factor, informing engineering decisions.
3. Constraints ensure that all optimized designs satisfy sliding, overturning, and bearing capacity requirements.

## Code Summary

- Trained a surrogate model for retaining wall analysis, performed multi-objective optimization, and generated Pareto-optimal design solutions

## Geotechnical Application

- Optimizing retaining wall geometry and reinforcement using ML surrogates for rapid design space exploration
