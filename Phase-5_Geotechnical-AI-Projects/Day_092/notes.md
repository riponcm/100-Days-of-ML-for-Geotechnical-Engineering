## Concepts Covered

- Slope stability analysis: factor of safety concepts
- Traditional methods: Bishop, Janbu, Spencer
- ML models for rapid factor of safety estimation
- Feature engineering from slope geometry and soil properties
- Sensitivity analysis for critical stability parameters

## Key Takeaways

1. ML models can predict slope stability factors of safety orders of magnitude faster than iterative limit equilibrium methods.
2. Trained on diverse slope configurations, ML models generalize across different geometries and soil conditions.
3. Sensitivity analysis identifies which parameters (cohesion, friction angle, slope angle, water table) most influence stability.

## Code Summary

- Built ML models for slope factor of safety prediction, validated against limit equilibrium results, and performed parametric sensitivity studies

## Geotechnical Application

- Rapid assessment of slope stability for preliminary design and screening of large numbers of slope configurations
