## Concepts Covered

- Ground motion selection for dynamic analysis
- Feature extraction from acceleration time histories
- Clustering ground motions by spectral shape and intensity measures
- Representative record selection from clusters
- Validation against target spectra and hazard-consistent selection

## Key Takeaways

1. Clustering ground motions by their characteristics enables systematic selection of representative records for dynamic analysis.
2. Feature-based clustering captures spectral shape, duration, and frequency content simultaneously.
3. Selected records should match target spectra and represent the range of ground motion characteristics expected at the site.

## Code Summary

- Extracted features from a ground motion database, clustered records by characteristics, and selected representative suites for site-specific analysis

## Geotechnical Application

- Selecting appropriate ground motion records for nonlinear dynamic analysis of geotechnical structures (dams, embankments, foundations)
