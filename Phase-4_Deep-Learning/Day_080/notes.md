## Concepts Covered

- Attention mechanism: query, key, value paradigm
- Self-attention and scaled dot-product attention
- Multi-head attention for capturing different relationships
- Transformer architecture: encoder and decoder blocks
- Positional encoding for sequence order

## Key Takeaways

1. Attention allows models to focus on the most relevant parts of the input, regardless of their distance in the sequence.
2. Self-attention captures pairwise relationships between all elements, making it powerful for finding patterns in sequential data.
3. Transformers have become the dominant architecture for sequence modeling, surpassing RNNs in many tasks.

## Geotechnical Application

- Applying attention mechanisms to geotechnical time series and sequential data where long-range dependencies are important
