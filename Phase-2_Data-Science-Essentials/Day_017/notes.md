## Concepts Covered

- Matrix operations: dot product, transpose, inverse, determinant
- Eigenvalue decomposition and its geometric interpretation
- Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) for matrix factorization
- Solving systems of linear equations with NumPy
- Condition number and numerical stability

## Key Takeaways

1. Eigenvalues reveal the principal directions and magnitudes of stress/strain tensors, directly applicable to soil mechanics.
2. SVD provides a robust way to decompose data matrices and is the foundation for PCA-based dimensionality reduction.
3. NumPy's linalg module offers production-ready implementations of core linear algebra operations needed in engineering analysis.

## Code Summary

- Computed eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a 2D stress tensor matrix, performed SVD on a geotechnical dataset, and solved equilibrium equations using np.linalg.solve

## Geotechnical Application

- Eigenvalue analysis of stress tensors to determine principal stresses in soil elements, and SVD for reducing high-dimensional site investigation data
